# Design direction — <product>

- **Audience & context:** who, where, on what device, in what mood
- **Personality (3 words):** e.g. "calm, precise, warm"
- **References:** 2–4 real products/sites and what specifically to borrow from each
- **Anti-references:** what this must not look like (e.g. generic SaaS dashboard)
- **Typography:** display / text / mono families, scale ratio, line-heights
- **Color:** tokens (bg, surface, text, muted, accent, danger, success) light + dark, contrast checked
- **Spacing & layout:** base unit, grid, density, max widths
- **Shape & depth:** radii, borders vs shadows
- **Motion:** durations, easing, what animates and what never does (respect reduced motion)
- **Components:** library choice (or custom) and why
- **States:** loading, empty, error, success patterns
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA target, focus style, hit areas
